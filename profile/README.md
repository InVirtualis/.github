In Virtualis builds **CARLA**, the intent layer between a brain-computer interface and the world. It works out what a person means from simple interactions, and acts on a single yes. You decide where to go; CARLA decides how to get there.

**Any input:** eyes, a ring, a headset, an implant. **Any output:** a screen, a prosthetic, a smart home, a robot.
